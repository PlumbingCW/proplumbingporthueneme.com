Plumbing Service Area: Port Hueneme, Oxnard and Channel Islands Beach

We are based in Port Hueneme and dispatch to the city and the two communities that touch it. Here is where we go and what is different in each.

Call 24/7: (805) 249-7135

Three places, one 24/7 line

Port Hueneme is the home base, ZIP code 93041, with 93044 used for post office boxes. Oxnard surrounds it and Channel Islands Beach sits near the harbor. All three get the same emergency line and the same pricing approach, but the water, the housing and the utility contacts differ.

Oxnard

Primary ZIP 93033, plus 93030 and 93036. The city reports harder water than Port Hueneme, with an average near 419 milligrams per liter.

Channel Islands Beach

ZIP 93035. The Community Services District runs water and sewer, so utility problems go to the district.

Port Hueneme: where we are based

Our address is 309 W Channel Islands Blvd, Port Hueneme, CA 93041. The City of Port Hueneme runs water, sewer collection and permits for the city. The Water Division is at 805-986-6563, the Wastewater Division at 805-986-6561 and Building and Safety at 805-986-6512.

Within the city we cover every neighborhood, from the streets near Hueneme Beach Park to the blocks near Bubbling Springs Park and the Naval Base Ventura County gate. We treat failed water heaters, blocked drains and hidden leaks as the most common calls.

How dispatch works across the three areas

One phone line covers Port Hueneme, Oxnard and Channel Islands Beach. When you call, give the dispatcher the street address and the nearest cross street, because the three communities share borders and some streets change jurisdiction block by block.

The dispatcher also asks what you can see and whether any water or gas is involved. That decides whether the call is routed as an emergency and what you should shut off while you wait.

Planned work such as a water heater install or a repipe is scheduled for a day that suits you. We write down the scope and the price before work starts.

Which utility to call for what

Plumbing trouble on your side of the meter or the property line is ours to fix. A main break in the street, low pressure across a whole block or a sewer main backup that reaches several homes belongs to the utility that serves your address.

That is why the table above lists who runs water in each place. If you are not sure which side the problem is on, a camera inspection or a pressure check will show it, and we tell you plainly when the next call should go to the utility instead of us.

What to do before we arrive

Close the main water valve for a leak, stop water use during a backup and keep the area clear so the plumber can get to the problem quickly.

If your address is on the edge

Some streets sit close to a boundary. Call with the street name and the nearest cross street, and the dispatcher will tell you honestly whether the address is within our dispatch area and which utility serves it.

Why local conditions matter

Water hardness, housing age and soil all differ a little between these places. Harder water means more scale in heaters and fixtures, and older homes mean more galvanized or aging copper lines.

Our leak detection and drain cleaning work account for those differences, which is why each location page spells out its own details instead of repeating one script.

Questions about where we work

Port Hueneme 93041 and 93044, Oxnard 93033, 93030 and 93036, and Channel Islands Beach 93035. If your address is near a boundary, call and give the street.

Pricing depends on the problem, access, parts and the hour of the call. We explain the factors before work starts, and the dispatcher can discuss travel when you call.

The City of Port Hueneme Water Division serves Port Hueneme, the City of Oxnard Water Division serves Oxnard and the Channel Islands Beach Community Services District serves its own area.

Call and tell us the address. If it is within a reasonable dispatch distance, we will say so honestly.
