What to have ready

The dispatcher moves faster when you can answer a few questions. None of this is required, and if you are in the middle of an emergency, just call.

  • Your street address and the nearest cross street.
  • What you see, hear or smell, and where.
  • Whether you have closed the main water valve.
  • The age and fuel of your water heater, if that is the problem.
  • Whether you rent, so we know who to speak with about approval.

If you smell gas, leave the building before you call. SoCalGas takes gas leak calls at 1-800-427-2200.
