No hot water: what usually failed

Most no-heat calls come down to a short list. On a gas heater the thermocouple, the pilot assembly or the gas control valve is the usual suspect. On an electric heater it is a heating element or a tripped high-limit switch. We test before we replace anything, so the part we install is the part that was bad.

A leaking tank versus a leaking fitting

Water on the floor is not always a dead tank. A weeping drain valve, a loose supply connector or a dripping relief valve can all look the same from across the room, and each is a short repair. We dry the area, trace the water upward, and find where it starts.

If the water is coming through the tank shell, the steel has corroded through and no repair will hold. We say so right away and explain the replacement options on our water heater replacement page, because paying for a repair on a failed tank wastes your money.

Hard water and the tank in your garage

Port Hueneme tap water is supplied by the city, from a blend of treated local groundwater and imported Calleguas water. The city 2025 water quality report lists total hardness at 210 parts per million, which is roughly 12 grains per gallon by our conversion. Hard water leaves scale on heating surfaces and settles as sediment in the bottom of the tank.

In practice that means a heater here benefits from a periodic flush, and sediment is a common reason a tank rumbles, heats slowly or wastes gas. Ask us about flushing at the same visit if the unit is more than a few years old and has never been drained.

What affects the cost of a water heater repair

Repair prices depend on what failed and how hard it is to reach. These five things set the price.

The failed part

A thermocouple or a relief valve costs far less than a gas control valve or a heating element pair.

Gas or electric

Gas work includes safety checks on venting and combustion air. Electric work includes testing the circuit.

Age and condition of the tank

On an old tank, a repair may only delay a leak. We tell you when that is likely.

Access and location

A garage heater is simple. A closet or attic unit takes longer and may need a second person.

After-hours dispatch

Night and weekend calls carry a premium over a scheduled weekday visit.

Insurance generally covers a sudden tank burst that damages the home, but not the cost of the heater itself or slow leaks from long-term corrosion. Photograph any water damage before cleanup.

You get a clear price before work starts. Call (805) 249-7135 with the make, age and symptoms and we can often say on the phone whether repair makes sense.

Repair or replace: a simple test

Tank heaters commonly last eight to twelve years. If yours is past that range and the repair cost is a large share of a new unit, replacement usually makes more sense. If it is under eight years and the fault is a single part, repair is nearly always the better choice.

  • Under eight years old, no water coming from the tank shell: repair.
  • Ten years or older with a second failure in two years: compare replacement cost.
  • Rust-colored water from the hot side only, or moisture at the tank base: plan for replacement.
  • Unit is a tankless model: see our tankless water heater page for flush and repair details.

Earthquake strapping and code

California Health and Safety Code section 19211 requires water heaters to be braced, anchored or strapped against earthquake movement, and the seller must certify that on a home sale. When we replace a part that requires moving the unit, or install a new one, we check the strapping and correct it. A loose heater is a gas and water hazard in a shake.

For work beyond repair, the City of Port Hueneme Building and Safety Division at City Hall, 250 N Ventura Road, handles permits. Permit rules vary, so ask the building department whether your specific job needs one. We can walk you through what we plan to do so you can ask the right question.
