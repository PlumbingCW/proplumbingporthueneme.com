Who runs water and sewer here

Channel Islands Beach has its own Community Services District, which provides water distribution, sewer service and refuse collection. The district office is at 353 Santa Monica Drive and the phone is (805) 985-6021. If water is bubbling up in the street, a manhole is overflowing or the public main is the problem, call the district. If the trouble is on the property side of the connection, call us.

The district buys treated water through the Port Hueneme Water Agency, which blends local groundwater with imported State Water. The district reports the blended source water at about 196 milligrams per liter of hardness, which is roughly 11.5 grains per gallon by our conversion. The district does not publish a tap-water hardness figure for its own distribution system, so we treat that number as source water and not a measure at your faucet.
