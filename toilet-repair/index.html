A toilet that overflows or leaks onto the floor needs attention right away. We stop the water, fix the cause and replace the toilet when repair no longer makes sense.

A toilet that is overflowing, leaking at the floor or running without stopping cannot wait for a business day. Our line is staffed 24/7, and a plumber can usually reach a Port Hueneme address the same day.

If it is overflowing, lift the tank lid and press the flapper down, or close the supply valve behind the toilet by turning it clockwise. Do not flush again. Mop up with towels, and call if the water does not stop or comes back.

Toilet overflowing: what to do first

  1. Stop flushing and lift the tank lid.
  2. Push the flapper down to stop water entering the bowl or close the supply valve at the wall.
  3. Put towels around the base and keep the area dry for safe walking.
  4. Do not use the washing machine or run other fixtures if the toilet is backing up from the line.
  5. Call and tell the dispatcher whether other drains are slow.

A toilet that overflows once and clears is usually a local clog. If the tub or other drains also gurgle or back up, the problem is further down the line, and we move to the main-line steps on our drain cleaning page.

Flange and floor damage

If a toilet has leaked for a while, the closet flange and subfloor can suffer. A rocking toilet or a soft floor near it is a sign the leak has been running longer than it looked. We check the flange and the floor when we pull the toilet, and tell you if the subfloor needs attention before we set a new toilet.

Replacing a toilet and California fixture rules

State law sets the water use of new toilets. Assembly Bill 715 requires high-efficiency toilets at 1.28 gallons per flush or less, in effect since January 1, 2014. Senate Bill 407 defines a toilet as noncompliant if it uses more than 1.6 gallons per flush in buildings built on or before January 1, 1994, set replacement deadlines of January 1, 2017 for single-family homes and January 1, 2019 for multifamily and commercial buildings, and requires seller disclosure.

In practice, if you are replacing an old toilet, you will be installing a compliant model. We help you choose a size and height that suit the room and the users, and we haul away the old one.

What affects the price of toilet repair or installation

A toilet job is either a small part or a full replacement. These factors set the price.

Repair or replacement

A flapper or fill valve is inexpensive. A new toilet includes the fixture and the install.

Condition of the flange and floor

A damaged flange or soft subfloor adds work.

Toilet model

Standard, comfort-height, one-piece and wall-hung models differ in price and install time.

Shutoff valve condition

A seized or leaking angle stop may need replacing at the same visit.

Time of day

Emergency dispatch costs more than a scheduled appointment.
