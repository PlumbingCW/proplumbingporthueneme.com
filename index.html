Emergency plumbing: what to do before we arrive

A plumbing emergency is any problem that is damaging the house or cannot wait for tomorrow. A burst pipe, sewage backing up, a tank leaking onto the floor and a gas smell all qualify. The first minutes matter more than the plumber, so start with these steps while you dial.

Water where it should not be

Close the main water valve, open a low faucet to drain the line and stay out of rooms where water reaches outlets. Then see burst pipe repair or leak detection.

Drains backing up

Stop all water use, keep children and pets away from sewage and skip chemical cleaners. Our drain cleaning and sewer line repair crews bring cable machines and cameras.

Smell of gas

Leave the building first. Call SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200 from a safe place, and 911 if anyone may be in danger. Call us after the utility clears the home.

Plumbing in Port Hueneme starts with the city's water

Port Hueneme is a small coastal city of about 21,000 people. Its Water Division serves roughly 6,500 connections with water from the Port Hueneme Water Agency plant, which blends treated local groundwater with imported Calleguas water. The 2025 city report lists hardness at 210 parts per million and disinfection with chloramines.

Hard water leaves scale on heating elements and heat exchangers. If your water heater rumbles or a tankless unit throws error codes, start with water heater repair or ask about water softening and filtration.

City housing records put about two in three homes as built before 1980, and about half of households rent. Older lines mean roots, cracks and worn valves. When a tenant calls us, we describe what we found in writing so the owner can act.

How emergency plumbing is priced

We do not post flat prices, because a drain clog, a failed tank and a broken pipe are different jobs. You get a clear price before work starts. The things that move the number are the problem itself, access to the pipe or heater, the parts and the hour of the call. Night, weekend and holiday dispatch costs more than a weekday visit.

Insurance often covers sudden water damage but not the repair of worn plumbing, so photograph damage and call your agent early. Each service page lists its own cost factors, including water heater replacement, hydro jetting and repiping.
