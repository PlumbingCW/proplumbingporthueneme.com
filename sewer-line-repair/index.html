Emergency Sewer Line Repair in Port Hueneme, CA

When cleaning no longer helps, the pipe itself needs attention. We find the failure on camera, explain your choices, and repair the section that is broken.

Call 24/7: (805) 249-7135

A failing sewer line shows itself as repeat backups, slow drains in several fixtures, gurgling toilets and, in the worst cases, sewage in the tub. Our emergency line is open 24/7 and a plumber can reach Port Hueneme addresses the same day in most cases.

Until we arrive, use as little water as possible, keep children and pets out of any wet area, and do not run the washer or dishwasher. If sewage is overflowing outside or from a manhole, report it to the city overflow line at 805-986-6556.

Sewer backup: what to do first

Stop the water

Every flush and every drain adds to the backup. Turn off the washing machine and dishwasher and hold off on showers.

Protect people

The county advises keeping people and pets, especially children, out of the affected area and washing hands after contact.

Call the right party

A clog in your own line is yours. A blockage in the city main is the city. We help tell the difference. Overflows go to 805-986-6556.

Document it

Photograph the water line and the damaged items. Insurers want proof of when and where the backup happened.

Who owns the pipe

The City of Port Hueneme owns and maintains the public sewer collection system. Under the city code as posted, the property owner maintains the connection between the building and the city sewer, up to and including the wye or saddle where it joins the main. Code language can change, so call the Wastewater Division at 805-986-6561 if you need to confirm who is responsible for a specific blockage.

That means a failing line from your house to the main is the owner's repair. For rentals, tenants and owners share some responsibility under the code, so a landlord and tenant should talk early. We write up what we find in plain terms so either party can use it.

Finding the failure first

We run a camera through the line before choosing a repair. The video shows whether the problem is roots, a crack, an offset joint, a belly that holds water or a collapse. Without that, a repair is a guess. If you would like to see the pipe before deciding, our sewer inspection service is built for that.

Repair methods

  • Spot repair: open a short section, replace the broken pipe and backfill.
  • Trenchless lining: insert a resin-saturated liner that hardens inside the old pipe, avoiding a long trench where the pipe is suitable.
  • Pipe bursting or pulling: a new pipe is drawn in as the old one is broken up, where access and conditions allow.
  • Full replacement: dig and re-lay the full lateral when the pipe is badly broken or has multiple failures.

No method fits every pipe. Lining needs a pipe that is not collapsed, and open excavation is hard under a driveway or a patio. We explain which methods apply to your line and leave the decision to you.

What affects the price of sewer line repair

Sewer repairs span a very wide range, and the cause of that range is easy to explain.

Length of pipe affected

A single short section costs less than a long run from the house to the main.

Depth and surface above the pipe

Digging through landscaping is cheaper than breaking a driveway or a patio.

Method chosen

Lining, bursting and excavation differ in cost, speed and disruption.

Permits and city work

Connections at the main may require city involvement. Contractors working in Port Hueneme need a city business license.

Time of day

Emergency dispatch costs more than a scheduled appointment.

After a sewage backup

Ventura County guidance for indoor sewage cleanup says to keep people out until the backup has stopped, discard saturated carpet and padding, and disinfect tools and hard surfaces. It also tells customers on a municipal sewer to contact their utility. Wear gloves, wash up, and do not bring floodwater near food.
