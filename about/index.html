About Port Hueneme Pro Plumbing

We answer plumbing emergencies in Port Hueneme, Oxnard and Channel Islands Beach. This page explains how we work and how you can check us.

Call 24/7: (805) 249-7135

Who we are

Port Hueneme Pro Plumbing is a 24/7 plumbing dispatch and service company based at 309 W Channel Islands Blvd in Port Hueneme. We handle emergency repairs and planned work for houses, rentals and small businesses in Port Hueneme, Oxnard and Channel Islands Beach.

We are licensed and insured. We do not publish a license number on a web page template, because a number only means something when you check it yourself. The state keeps a public lookup, and we encourage you to use it on any plumber you consider.

How a call is handled

The line is staffed around the clock. The dispatcher asks for your street, what you see and whether water or gas is involved, then tells you what to shut off while the plumber is on the way. We offer same-day appointments when available and we do not promise a fixed arrival window, because traffic and open calls change by the hour.

Once the plumber has looked at the problem, you get a clear price before work starts. You decide whether to go ahead. If the better answer is a different service, such as replacing a tank instead of repairing it, we say so.

What we rely on

Our pages cite the City of Port Hueneme water quality report, the municipal code as posted, state agencies such as the Contractors State License Board and the gas utility SoCalGas. When we cannot find a rule on a primary source, we write that we could not find it. That includes things like who owns the water service line between the meter and the house and whether a lateral inspection is required at sale.

Our service pages explain what changes the price of each job, and our blog covers the questions people ask before they call.

What we will and will not do

We will tell you when a repair is the wrong answer. A tank that is leaking from the shell cannot be repaired, and a supply line with many pinholes will keep failing, so we say so and show the options.

We will not invent a deadline to push you into work. We will not quote an unrelated discount or promise a fixed arrival time, and we will not claim credentials we cannot show you.

Who we serve

Most calls come from homeowners and renters, with a steady share from small businesses such as shops, restaurants and offices. The same dispatch line handles both, and commercial plumbing calls get the same first-step guidance as a house call.

Property managers and landlords can ask for a written summary after each visit. That record helps with tenants, insurers and later repairs.

Our approach to pricing

Plumbing prices depend on access, parts, the age of the system and the hour of the call, so a flat list on a web page would mislead you. Each service page explains the factors that move the number for that job.

You hear the price before work starts and you can say no. If the plumber finds a second problem while working, the new work is priced and approved separately before it begins.

Keep the written summary we give you. It records what was found, what was done and which parts were used, and that helps if you sell the house or file an insurance claim later.

How to reach us

Call (805) 249-7135 at any hour. Our address is 309 W Channel Islands Blvd, Port Hueneme, CA 93041, and the email address is contact@proplumbingporthueneme.com for non-urgent questions. Anything urgent should be a phone call, not an email.

Checking a plumber

California requires a license for plumbing work that needs a permit or costs $1,000 or more in labor and materials. Plumbers working in Port Hueneme also need a city business license. Ask to see both, get the scope and price in writing, and be wary of anyone who asks for a large payment up front.
