Emergency Hydro Jetting in Port Hueneme, CA

A cable punches a hole through a clog. A jetter washes the whole pipe. When drains keep coming back, high-pressure water is the fix that lasts.

Call 24/7: (805) 249-7135

Hydro jetting is the heavy option for a line that has blocked more than once. It sends water at high pressure through a hose and a nozzle that fires jets backward and forward, scouring the pipe wall instead of just boring a channel through the middle. Our dispatch line is staffed 24/7 for backups that cannot wait.

If sewage is rising in a tub or floor drain right now, stop using every fixture, keep children and pets out of the wet area, and call. A first pass with a cable may open the line so that jetting can be scheduled for the clean-up.

Sewer backup right now: what to do first

  • Stop running water, flushing and using the washing machine or dishwasher.
  • Keep people and pets away from standing sewage and wash hands after any contact.
  • Switch off power in a wet area only if you can reach the breaker from a dry spot.
  • Call and describe which fixtures are backing up. The lowest drain in the house usually shows the problem first.
  • Report a street-side overflow or a manhole spill to the city at 805-986-6556.

Most plumbing calls start with a cable because it is quick and cheap. Jetting enters the picture when the same line fills again, when a camera shows a coated wall, or when the clog is a mix of grease and roots that a cable keeps tearing through without removing.

What jetting can and cannot do

Water pressure removes soft and semi-hard buildup well. It does not repair a crack, close an offset joint or restore a collapsed section. If a camera shows structural damage, pushing high pressure into that spot can make it worse, so we look first. Our sewer camera inspection shows the pipe and tells us which tool is safe.

Roots are the other limit. A jetter with a cutting nozzle clears roots that have grown into a joint, but they return unless the joint is sealed or the section is lined or replaced. We say so up front and leave the choice to you.

What affects the price of hydro jetting

Jetting is priced by the job, not by a posted rate. These factors set the number.

Length and size of the line

A short branch costs less than a long main with several bends.

How heavy the buildup is

A coated line may need more than one pass or a second nozzle.

Access

A working cleanout keeps the job simple. No cleanout means more setup.

Camera before or after

A camera check before jetting protects the pipe. One after shows the result.

Time of day

Night and weekend dispatch costs more than a weekday appointment.

Restaurants, food service and grease lines

Port Hueneme runs a Fats, Oils and Grease program, and food businesses must keep grease devices serviced and keep hauler receipts. Kitchen drain lines inside a restaurant fill with grease faster than any house line. Jetting is the standard way to clean them, and it fits alongside commercial plumbing service for after-hours work.

Older lines and what we look for

By the city's own housing records, most Port Hueneme homes predate 1980, which means lines with decades of buildup and rough joints. We pair jetting with a look inside the pipe whenever the history suggests damage. If you also have a drain cleaning problem at a single fixture, a smaller machine often handles it without jetting.
