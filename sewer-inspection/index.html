Sewer Camera Inspection in Port Hueneme, CA

A camera turns guesses into evidence. We feed a waterproof camera through the line, show you what is there, and give you a written summary you can use.

Call 24/7: (805) 249-7135

A sewer camera inspection is the planned way to learn the condition of a line without digging. A small waterproof camera on a flexible cable travels through the pipe while a monitor shows the picture. We use it before repairs, before home purchases and when a drain keeps clogging for no clear reason.

Most inspections are scheduled, but if the line is backing up now, it is a problem for our drain cleaning or repair service first. A camera cannot see through standing sewage.

When a camera inspection makes sense

Before a purchaseKnow the condition of the lateral
After repeat clogsFind the cause, not just the symptom
Before repairChoose the right method

Buyers get the most from an inspection. A sewer line that looks fine from a house walkthrough can be cracked or full of roots underneath. Owners with repeat clogs get the second benefit, since a camera shows whether the pipe is simply dirty or actually damaged. Planners of remodels, additions and new fixtures use the third, because the line has to carry the added load.

What we do on the visit

  1. We find the cleanout or the nearest access point and clear any standing debris so the camera can pass.
  2. We feed the camera through the line, watching the monitor and noting distance and depth where we see something.
  3. We record the video so you can see the same footage and keep a copy.
  4. We explain what we found in plain words and give you a written summary of defects and recommendations.

We may locate a defect at the surface with a receiver, so you know exactly where to dig if repair is needed. That step avoids opening the wrong spot.

What affects the price of a camera inspection

We quote this by the job, not from a posted price list. The variables are few.

Access to the line

A clear cleanout is simple. No cleanout means we may need to pull a toilet or work from a roof vent.

Length of the line

A long run to the street takes longer than a short branch.

Cleaning needed first

A heavily coated line needs cleaning before the camera sees anything.

Locating and marking

Surface locating adds time and gives you a precise dig point.

Report and video copy

A written summary and a copy of the recording are part of what you receive.

Why local conditions matter

City records put roughly 65 percent of housing units as built before 1980, and older houses are more likely to have original clay, cast iron or early plastic lines. Those lines can crack, separate or collect roots. The property owner maintains the private connection up to the city sewer under the code as posted, so the condition of that pipe is a purchase and ownership matter.
